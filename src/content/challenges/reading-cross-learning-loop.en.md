## A practical loop

1. Track repeat tasks
2. Write a prompt or brief for one of them
3. Upgrade it into a tool-assisted workflow
4. Turn it into a Skill or SOP
5. Add regression cases and safety boundaries

## Why this works

You are not memorizing prompt tricks.  
You are gradually structuring real work so it becomes reusable, transferable, and safer.

## The endpoint is not a product

Doubao-style chat, Cursor, and OpenClaw are just interfaces.  
The durable skill is learning how to describe work, connect resources, constrain execution, and verify results.
