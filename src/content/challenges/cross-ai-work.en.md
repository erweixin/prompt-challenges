## Three separate questions

1. **Capability**: When can models/tools cover the core outputs of my role? (Changes as tech moves.)
2. **Handoff**: Am I willing—and allowed—to delegate decisions and accountability?
3. **Competition**: Can others + AI replicate my visible deliverables more cheaply?

## Practical move

- Split your work into **highly repetitive** vs **judgment-heavy** tasks; automate the former first, keep humans in the loop for the latter.
- Write **acceptance criteria** before talking about “full automation.”
