## One conversation, one job

Split “research + draft + translate” into separate threads—often **fewer hallucinations** and **lower token spend** (long history costs more and drags context).

## Be precise early

State **goal, audience, format, constraints, acceptance** before iterating randomly.

## SOP → Skill

If you do it thrice, capture **steps + I/O + failures**; start with one hot path, not a giant library.

## Skip small talk with the model

Politeness belongs to humans; fluff burns context.
